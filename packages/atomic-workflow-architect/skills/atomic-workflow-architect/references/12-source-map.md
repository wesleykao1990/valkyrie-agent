## Source discipline

- Official docs/source establish available mechanisms and contracts.
- Transcript demonstrations establish usage patterns and creator intent, not guaranteed performance.
- Anecdotal cost, merge-rate, time-saving, and defect-catching claims remain hypotheses until reproduced.
- Static model tables are not permanent truth.
- Project-specific decisions come from Wesley's accepted architecture and measurements.
